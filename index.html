My Part-Time Job as a Dog Sitter

Hi! I'm Angelique and I work part-time as a dog sitter for Binbin (or what I call Bebu). I look after Bebu while Ate Maddie (his owner) is out of town, has work, or is just too busy to be at home. It all started when my older sister originally had the job. But ever since she got busy with her main job, she passed it on to me, and it became my part-time work. Every time I tell people about this, they get surprised because they have never heard of or even considered that this kind of job exists. Well, it does, and I earn a very good amount for a student while still enjoying the work I do.

Binbin, or Bebu, is a cute dog influencer. He has his own social media platform and business that many people adore. Many would think that he's scary because of his breed, but actually he is one of the sweetest dogs I have ever met. His size and looks might overwhelm other people and other dogs, but once they have bonded with him, they will realize that neither breed nor size should define their dog-sonality (personality, lol).

What I Usually Do

Here is what I do:

  • Dog Walking
  • Feeding and giving water
  • Overnight stays at the condo

This is my routine whenever the owner is not around:

  1. Go to the condo where Bebu lives.
  2. Ask ate Maddie if he has already eaten.
  3. Feed him if he hasn't yet, or take him out to the condo's garden if he has already eaten.
  4. Play with him until he gets tired.
  5. We both sleep.
  6. Repeat the next day.
